# Top 10 Hospitals in Mumbai for Surgical Care: Specialty Centers & Beds

In Mumbai, a hospital comparison is also a comparison of journeys, specialist pathways and the support available after discharge. A patient living in the western suburbs may find a central or south-Mumbai campus appropriate for a particular operation, yet still need a practical plan for repeated reviews. Someone travelling for cancer surgery may need pathology assessment and several specialties before a date can be offered.

This guide to top hospitals in Mumbai for surgery identifies ten institutions using their own published service information. It covers broad surgical hospitals and a dedicated cancer centre, making the differences in purpose explicit. The list is not a ranking of surgeons, treatment outcomes or value for money. A hospital's inclusion does not imply that every operation is available to every patient.

The roadmap title mentions specialty centres and beds. Rather than presenting uncertain capacity figures, the guide explains how to ask about the bed and monitoring arrangements your operation actually requires. Use it to shortlist a suitable service, prepare an informed consultation and compare written estimates without confusing an advertised package with an individual treatment plan.

## A service-based shortlist, not a performance league table

Research on 28 October 2026 checked official hospital pages for identity, locality and relevant services. Department descriptions were preferred when they provided a clearer explanation of clinical scope. The featured photograph is an AI-generated illustration of a generic reception, not a photograph of any institution below.

Comparable risk-adjusted results were not established for these ten institutions. We have therefore omitted numerical success ratings and claims that one offers better surgical outcomes. Current bed availability, tariffs, consultant schedules and insurer arrangements require direct confirmation. Accreditation logos on provider sites have not been independently checked against certificate scope and validity; they are not used to score the shortlist.

## Ten hospitals and the services their sources describe

### 1. Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital

**Location:** Four Bungalows, Andheri West. The [official hospital site](https://www.kokilabenhospital.com/) lists bone-and-joint, cancer, cardiac, neuroscience, bariatric, robotic and transplant centres, alongside critical care and rehabilitation. Its separate location page identifies the Andheri West address.

The range supports a specialty-led enquiry, rather than booking an unspecified surgical package. Tell the appointment team which diagnosis or proposed operation needs assessment. For an orthopaedic referral, for example, ask how rehabilitation will be coordinated; a cancer referral requires a different discussion about diagnosis and treatment sequence. Confirm that the consultant, investigations and admission are all at the intended campus before arranging accommodation nearby.

### 2. P. D. Hinduja Hospital and Medical Research Centre

**Location:** Mahim. Hinduja's [general-surgery service](https://www.hindujahospital.com/speciality/general-surgery/) describes open and minimally invasive surgery, a digestive-disease approach involving other specialties, and surgical oncology and trauma services. Its gastrointestinal surgery page separately identifies liver, biliary, pancreatic and bowel procedures.

This is useful for patients whose abdominal symptoms or findings need more than a straightforward operation booking. Ask whether general surgery or the specialist GI service should assess your case, and whether outside scans and biopsy material should be reviewed first. The hospital also has a Khar presence; use “Mahim” on appointment and estimate requests to prevent a branch assumption.

### 3. Lilavati Hospital and Research Centre

**Location:** Bandra Reclamation, Bandra West. [Lilavati's official website](https://www.lilavatihospital.com/) identifies gastrointestinal and colorectal surgery, neurosurgery, orthopaedics, gynaecology and liver-transplant services among its departments. It also provides appointment and estimate-request routes.

The practical advantage of a clear enquiry route is the ability to request information against a documented diagnosis. Send records only through an approved hospital channel, and ask which consultant will assess them. An estimate request should state the proposed operation and relevant assumptions rather than asking for the price of “surgery”. If the plan is not yet decided, treat the first visit as an evaluation and obtain cost details after the clinician explains the options.

### 4. Jaslok Hospital and Research Centre

**Location:** South Mumbai. Jaslok's [specialty directory](https://www.jaslokhospital.net/) includes general/laparoscopic, gastrointestinal, colorectal, cardiovascular/thoracic, robotic and spine surgery, with critical-care and other supporting services.

A directory containing several surgical routes can be helpful when there is a confirmed diagnosis, but confusing when symptoms have not yet been assessed. Describe the referral question accurately and ask which department is appropriate. If two specialists need to contribute, establish whether assessments can be coordinated and who will explain the combined recommendation. Ask the booking team to confirm the full address and building entry; do not assume that a listing under a familiar Mumbai locality is sufficient for arrival planning.

### 5. Sir H. N. Reliance Foundation Hospital and Research Centre

**Location:** Raja Rammohan Roy Road, Girgaon. Its [care-centres page](https://www.rfhospital.org/care-centres) describes diagnostic, critical-care, rehabilitation and specialty services. The hospital's technology page identifies laparoscopic and robotic platforms within general surgery.

For a patient comparing technology-led treatment, the next step is to ask why that approach is recommended for the specific illness. Request the alternative approach, the circumstances that might change the plan and the expected follow-up requirements. Patients coming from outside Mumbai should also discuss whether later care can be shared with a local doctor. A broad service list does not resolve those individual clinical or logistical questions.

### 6. Nanavati Max Super Speciality Hospital

**Location:** Vile Parle, Mumbai. The [hospital's official directory](https://www.nanavatimaxhospital.org/) distinguishes general/minimal-access surgery, robotic services, neurosciences, joint/spine care and surgical intensive care, alongside its cancer programmes.

These separate units make it worth checking who will lead the admission. A spine, abdominal or cancer referral should be routed to the relevant team, with a written account of the proposed treatment. Ask whether a quoted package includes the needed postoperative monitoring, implants or devices and rehabilitation. If the hospital describes new buildings or service expansion, confirm which unit will actually provide your care rather than assuming that every advertised facility forms part of the booking.

### 7. Bombay Hospital and Medical Research Centre

**Location:** Marine Lines. [Bombay Hospital's website](https://www.bombayhospital.com/) lists cardiovascular/thoracic surgery, neurosurgery, paediatric surgery, urology and surgical oncology, along with general/minimally invasive surgery and critical-care services.

The spread of specialties is relevant when a patient already has a referral needing a particular surgical discipline. For a child, specify that paediatric assessment is required; an adult surgical appointment is not an interchangeable substitute. Ask the team which reports it needs in advance and whether medical coexisting conditions require another consultation. Confirm the operative recommendation and expected support with the clinician before comparing the financial quote with a different institution.

### 8. Breach Candy Hospital Trust

**Location:** Bhulabhai Desai Road, south Mumbai. The [official site](https://www.breachcandyhospital.org/) lists general/laparoscopic surgery, orthopaedics, urology and cardiothoracic services, together with radiology and blood-bank contact routes.

When seeking an elective admission, first establish the admitting consultant and how the hospital coordinates the required assessments. A published department or contact number does not establish a bed reservation. Families should ask how room selection, any monitoring needs and admission timing interact. If an insurer is involved, confirm the hospital unit and proposed procedure in the approval request, and retain the estimate and authorisation separately so their different purposes remain clear.

### 9. Saifee Hospital

**Location:** Maharshi Karve Marg, Mumbai. [Saifee's official website](https://www.saifeehospital.com/) identifies medical/surgical specialties, orthopaedic services and a robotic surgery programme. Its published address distinguishes the hospital from similarly named listings elsewhere.

Patients should connect the technology description to the actual surgical team and indication. Ask whether the proposed operation is performed through open, laparoscopic or robotic access, and why. Check how much of the estimate is attributable to approach-specific equipment or consumables. If you need help moving around, discuss entry, discharge transport and caregiver support beforehand; those arrangements are practical questions for the hospital rather than facts established by this guide.

### 10. Tata Memorial Hospital

**Location:** Parel. The [Tata Memorial Centre website](https://tmc.gov.in/) identifies Tata Memorial Hospital separately from ACTREC in Navi Mumbai. It is a cancer-care institution; it should not be treated as a general destination for every non-cancer surgical condition. A WHO institutional profile also identifies its Parel location and cancer focus.

For a suspected or confirmed malignancy, ask about registration, pathology review and the relevant disease team. Bring the existing diagnosis and treatment records, and check whether original slides or blocks are requested. Confirm which campus will provide consultations and treatment. The Parel and Kharghar locations should not be conflated when organising accommodation, transport or follow-up.

## Why a bed count is not enough

An institution's total bed capacity does not tell you whether the bed required for your admission is available. It may include different wards, specialties or buildings. The meaningful question is where you are likely to recover and how a change in clinical needs would be managed.

Ask the surgeon and anaesthesia team whether the expected pathway involves day care, a ward stay or more intensive observation. Then confirm how the admission office handles that plan. Do not reserve an arbitrary higher room category on the assumption that it replaces clinical monitoring, and do not infer a fixed stay from another patient's experience.

Website capacity figures can also be inconsistent after expansion or restructuring. We have not reconciled a current, like-for-like bed dataset across these institutions and therefore do not reproduce one. Published infrastructure should support a conversation about care, not become an unsupported proxy for quality.

## Compare the total episode of care

Obtain a written estimate with the procedure name, access approach, room category and key assumptions. Request separate identification of professional fees, theatre/anaesthesia costs, implants, equipment consumables, medicines, investigations and possible monitoring charges. Clarify whether follow-up, dressings, physiotherapy or pathology are included.

Mumbai travel and accommodation may be a substantial part of an outstation family's overall budget. Ask how many appointments must be in person, whether records can be reviewed before travel and where a caregiver can wait during treatment. Do not book a fixed return journey on the basis of an informal recovery promise.

Confirm cashless arrangements through your insurer as well as the hospital. Network participation, procedure authorisation and the amount payable by the patient are different questions. No current comparable surgical tariffs were verified for this article. Written quotes prepared for the same clinical plan are more useful than invented city-wide price bands.

## Questions that reveal gaps in a proposed admission

1. What diagnosis and operation are recorded on the estimate?
2. Which surgeon will perform it, and who covers their absence?
3. Is another specialist opinion needed before a decision?
4. Where will postoperative recovery and monitoring occur?
5. Which costs change if the procedure or stay changes?
6. What records should accompany an outstation referral?
7. How is urgent reassessment arranged after discharge?
8. Can later follow-up be coordinated closer to home?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this a list of the ten safest hospitals in Mumbai?

No. The selection describes published services. It is not based on a common risk-adjusted surgical-outcomes audit and should not be read as a safety ranking or endorsement of a particular surgeon.

### Why include a dedicated cancer hospital in a surgical guide?

Some patients need cancer-specific assessment and treatment sequencing. Tata Memorial is included for that purpose. Its profile explicitly distinguishes that role from general surgical care for an unrelated condition.

### Does a larger bed count mean faster admission?

Not necessarily. The relevant specialty, monitoring needs, staffing and scheduled admissions determine availability. Ask the admissions team about your actual pathway after the specialist has assessed the case.

### Should I stay near the hospital after surgery?

Ask the treating team whether your circumstances require a local stay and when travel is appropriate. Consider the distance to urgent review, planned appointments and whether a caregiver can assist you.

### Are similarly named campuses interchangeable?

No. A brand can have more than one location or institution. Confirm the operating campus and full address on the appointment, estimate and insurance paperwork, particularly when a specialist works at several sites.

### How should I use online patient reviews?

They may suggest questions about communication, appointments or billing. Individual reviews cannot establish a comparative surgical success rate. Discuss clinical outcomes and complications with the responsible team using clearly defined measures.

### Can a hospital coordinator choose the operation for me?

An administrative discussion can organise appointments and costs, but the clinical assessment and consent discussion belong with the treating clinician. Ask to speak to the specialist before accepting a treatment-specific financial proposal.

### What is needed for a useful second opinion?

Bring the same diagnosis, imaging, pathology where relevant, medicines and previous treatment details to both consultations. Ask the second clinician to explain any difference in the diagnosis, urgency, approach or expected follow-up.

### Are current accreditation claims verified here?

No. Provider websites may display accreditation information, but this guide has not independently checked every active certificate. Ask for its date, campus and scope rather than treating a logo as a procedure-specific outcomes certificate.

## Make the next enquiry specific

Use this guide to top hospitals in Mumbai for surgery to select the right service and locality, then compare complete care plans. Record the operation proposed, the reason it is recommended, the support needed afterward and the estimate's assumptions. A sensible decision connects specialist suitability, clinical resources, financial clarity and workable follow-up. It does not depend on a promotional ranking or an isolated bed count.

Carry the same records to each opinion so recommendations can be compared fairly.
