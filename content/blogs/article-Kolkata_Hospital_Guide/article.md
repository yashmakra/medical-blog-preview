# Top 10 Hospitals in Kolkata for Surgical Care: Infrastructure & Doctor Profiles

A list of hospital names is only the beginning of a surgical decision. What matters next is whether the hospital has the team for the operation you need, how that team explains the choices and whether you can manage the follow-up. Patients comparing the top hospitals in Kolkata for surgery should first identify the diagnosis and the relevant surgical specialty.

Kolkata’s hospital options include dedicated programmes, multi-speciality centres and public teaching institutions. Some organisations operate several campuses, and older names may still appear in records or conversations. An appointment, estimate and admission letter should identify the same intended hospital. This is particularly useful when relatives are organising travel or seeking a second opinion from outside the city.

This guide provides ten sourced options in no performance order. It focuses on service scope, location and ways to evaluate specialist profiles. It does not rank hospitals by survival, star ratings or advertised procedure volumes. Use the comparison to prepare a consultation and ask for a written clinical and financial plan; urgent treatment should not be postponed to complete a routine shortlist.

## How the comparison was researched

Official hospital, institutional and hospital-hosted profile pages were checked on 28 October 2026 for a real location and surgical relevance. The list covers different surgical needs, so a cardiac centre should not be compared with a general-surgery service as though the operations were identical. Inclusion is not an endorsement.

We have not independently verified comparative outcomes, full current prices or accreditation validity across these campuses. Ask for current scope and certificates where relevant. A provider’s service page confirms what it advertises; it does not establish that a particular surgeon or operating slot is available for your case.

## Ten Kolkata hospitals to consider

### Apollo Multispeciality Hospitals

**Location:** Canal Circular Road, Kadapara, Kankurgachi.

Apollo’s Kolkata department lists general, gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary, hernia and emergency surgery. Its official contact page identifies Canal Circular Road; this is more precise than the common shorthand “Salt Lake”. Ask which surgical department treats your diagnosis and where the operation will occur. Broad website success figures without clear definitions are not used as comparative evidence here.

[Official information](https://www.apollohospitals.com/kolkata/departments/general-surgery).

### Fortis Hospital, Anandapur

**Location:** Anandapur, Kolkata.

The campus lists general and laparoscopic surgery, surgical oncology, cardiac surgery and other surgical specialties, with anaesthesia and critical-care support. Use the Anandapur identity when booking because Fortis also has a separate Kolkata kidney-focused hospital. Ask whether the responsible team has reviewed your existing records and which investigations are still needed before an operation is proposed.

[Official information](https://www.fortishealthcare.com/location/fortis-hospital-anandapur-kolkata).

### Manipal Hospitals, Dhakuria

**Location:** Gariahat Road, Dhakuria.

The Dhakuria service describes open and laparoscopic general surgery, with consultant profiles covering colorectal and gastrointestinal work. This location offers a distinct south-Kolkata consultation pathway. Ask whether the surgeon’s specific practice matches the operation being considered, and confirm admission and follow-up at this campus. A general surgical title alone does not demonstrate equal focus on every procedure.

[Official information](https://www.manipalhospitals.com/dhakuria/specialities/general-surgery/).

### Manipal Hospitals, Mukundapur

**Location:** Mukundapur, Kolkata.

Manipal maintains a separate Mukundapur general-surgery department page. Treat this as a different campus from Dhakuria, with its own appointment and admission arrangements. Ask the booking desk to confirm the current specialist, intended operating location and postoperative review site. Group membership should not be taken to mean that all teams, equipment and schedules are identical across hospitals.

[Official information](https://www.manipalhospitals.com/mukundapur/specialities/general-surgery/).

### Desun Hospital

**Location:** Desun More, EM Bypass, Kasba.

Desun’s general and laparoscopic service lists hernia repair, appendix and gallbladder procedures, with an official surgical roster. Its published address identifies the Kasba/EM Bypass location. Use the procedure list to ask what approach is suitable, rather than adopting promotional recovery promises. Clarify whether a planned day-care procedure could require overnight observation and how that would be managed.

[Official information](https://desunhospital.com/department/general-surgery/).

### Peerless Hospital and B. K. Roy Research Centre

**Location:** Panchasayar, Kolkata.

Peerless describes upper gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary-pancreatic, colorectal and laparoscopic surgery, with input from gastroenterology, radiology and pathology for complex cases. This is useful when a digestive condition needs more than one specialist’s assessment. Ask who coordinates the diagnostic findings and explains the final surgical recommendation. Confirm whether all proposed appointments can take place at the Panchasayar campus.

[Official information](https://www.peerlesshospital.com/general-surgery.php).

### The Calcutta Medical Research Institute, CMRI

**Location:** Diamond Harbour Road, New Alipore.

CMRI’s general-surgery department describes open and laparoscopic procedures, emergency work and gastrointestinal surgery. Its official contact information gives the Diamond Harbour Road address. Distinguish CMRI from the group’s separate BM Birla heart hospital. Ask whether the proposed surgery needs another specialty’s input and which consultant remains responsible if several departments participate.

[Official information](https://ckbirlahospitals.com/cmri/department-of-general-surgery).

### Woodlands Multispeciality Hospital

**Location:** Alipore Road, Alipore.

Woodlands’ current official site lists procedures including laparoscopic appendix and gallbladder surgery, hernia repair, thyroid and breast operations. Its hospital-hosted consultant directory provides a route to specialty appointments. Check current schedules directly rather than treating a downloadable timetable as a permanent promise. Ask which operation is proposed and what care after discharge is included in the plan.

[Official information](https://www.woodlandshospital.in/).

### Rabindranath Tagore International Institute of Cardiac Sciences

**Location:** Mukundapur, Kolkata.

Narayana’s official hospital page confirms this Mukundapur institution, while its current specialist profile establishes adult cardiac-surgical practice including aortic and minimally invasive work. A heart-surgery assessment follows a different pathway from routine abdominal surgery. Ask for the relevant cardiac team and a procedure-specific opinion. The institute’s name and a doctor’s broad interests do not establish a comparative outcome ranking.

[Official information](https://www.narayanahealth.org/c/kolkata/cardiac-surgery-adult-cardiac-surgery-paediatric/dr-lalit-kapoor).

### IPGME&R and SSKM Hospital

**Location:** A. J. C. Bose Road, Kolkata.

The government institution lists general surgery, cardiothoracic and vascular surgery and other specialties. Its citizen charter describes access to hospital services. This offers a public teaching-hospital option alongside private providers. Ask about current registration, the appropriate surgical unit, referral requirements and the location of the relevant clinic; the institution also has annex hospitals with separate addresses.

[Official information](https://www.ipgmer.gov.in/).

## Read doctor profiles for relevance

Profiles are useful when they describe a current hospital appointment, qualifications and the procedures a clinician focuses on. They become less useful when broad adjectives replace a clear scope of practice. Ask the hospital to confirm that the clinician you meet is the person expected to operate, or explain any shared-team arrangement.

For example, Manipal’s official Dhakuria profile identifies Dr Tanmoy Mukherjee as a general-surgery consultant with colorectal and laparoscopic practice. CMRI lists Dr Abhishek Bhartia as a general and advanced laparoscopic surgeon. Narayana identifies Dr Lalit Kapoor as a senior adult cardiac-surgery consultant at RN Tagore. These are examples of different relevant practices, not recommendations or a ranking of doctors. Reconfirm current appointments directly.

Avoid using years in practice as the only comparison. Ask how the proposed surgeon assesses your particular condition, what alternatives are reasonable and how complications or an unexpected change in the operation would be managed. A second opinion should explain differences in diagnosis or planning rather than simply endorse a brand.

## Ask what the infrastructure means for your operation

A modular theatre, ICU, endoscopy suite or robotic platform has a role only in relation to the procedure and patient. Ask which support services are actually expected to be needed and whether they are available at the admission campus. For a complex condition, ask how radiology, pathology, anaesthesia and other specialists communicate their findings.

Preoperative tests should have a purpose. NICE guidance on routine testing distinguishes the operation and the patient’s health rather than treating every test as necessary for everyone. Ask what each proposed test will clarify, whether a recent result can be used and who reviews it. Do not omit a test on your own; resolve the question with the treating team.

## Costs, insurance and admission details

There is no universal Kolkata surgical price in this guide. A small day-care procedure, cancer resection and heart operation involve different care. Ask for an estimate tied to the diagnosis, proposed procedure, campus and room category, with its date and validity period recorded.

Check surgeon and anaesthetist fees, theatre costs, consumables, implants where relevant, medicines, investigations, pathology and expected stay. Ask which review visits are included and what happens if observation or admission lasts longer. Compare the same scenario at each hospital; otherwise a lower total may simply omit something another estimate includes.

Verify insurer or scheme eligibility through the authorised hospital desk and the insurer or TPA. Ask about pre-authorisation, room restrictions, co-payment, excluded charges and deposits. For a public hospital, clarify registration and any applicable eligibility documentation separately rather than assuming all treatments or supplies are automatically free.

## Questions to take to the hospital

- Which specialty matches the proposed operation?
- What does the current profile establish?
- Which campus resources are needed?
- How are different specialist opinions coordinated?
- Who will explain the final diagnosis and next step?

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this guide identify Kolkata’s highest-rated surgical hospital?

No. It provides a non-ranked, source-based shortlist. The relevant comparison depends on the operation, the team and your health needs, rather than a general online score.

### Is Apollo’s listed campus actually on Canal Circular Road?

Yes. Its official contact page gives the Kadapara/Kankurgachi address. Use that full address instead of relying only on informal area descriptions when arranging transport or sending records.

### Are Manipal Dhakuria and Mukundapur one campus?

No. They have separate department and appointment pages. Confirm where your consultation, investigations, operation and reviews are planned.

### Why include a cardiac institute in a broad surgery guide?

Cardiac surgery is one surgical pathway, and RN Tagore has official evidence of that practice. It should be compared with suitable cardiac services for a heart operation, not treated as interchangeable with general surgery.

### Are the named doctor examples endorsements?

No. They illustrate how official profiles describe different surgical practices. Appointments and responsibilities must be confirmed with the hospital before care is arranged.

### Can a downloadable consultant timetable be relied on?

Use it as a starting point. Schedules and appointments can change, so confirm the current clinic and operating arrangements directly.

### What should I bring to a second consultation?

Bring investigation reports, original images where available, prior treatment notes and medicines. Ask whether pathology slides or other material are required for the particular diagnosis.

### Should every operation have the same preoperative tests?

No. The operation, anaesthetic and health assessment influence testing. Ask the clinical team what is needed and why, rather than copying another patient’s investigation list.

### How should an outstation patient plan follow-up?

Discuss the likely review needs before admission. Ask when travel may be suitable, which visits require examination and whether any care can be coordinated with a clinician nearer home.

## Choose the relevant specialist pathway

The top hospitals in Kolkata for surgery are most usefully compared after the procedure is defined. Check the correct campus, a relevant current specialist and the services the operation requires. Then compare written estimates and follow-up arrangements so the decision reflects the patient’s needs and a plan the family understands.
