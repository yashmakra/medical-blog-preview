# Top 10 Hospitals in Ahmedabad for Surgical Care: Facilities & Specialists

Ahmedabad has hospital options for a simple day-care procedure, complex abdominal surgery, cardiac surgery and many other operations. Those options should not be compared as though “surgery” were one treatment. Before evaluating the top hospitals in Ahmedabad for surgery, ask your clinician to name the diagnosis, the procedure being considered and the specialty that should assess it.

The city’s hospital names also cover separate campuses with different roles. A consultation centre may not be the place where an operation is performed, and a cancer centre may be separate from a group’s multi-specialty hospital. For families travelling from elsewhere in Gujarat, those distinctions affect accommodation, transport and repeated review visits as well as the admission itself.

This guide offers ten sourced institutions or campuses in no performance order. It includes public teaching services, multi-specialty hospitals and a dedicated cardiac institute, with their different roles explained. Use it to prepare a relevant consultation and request a written clinical and financial plan. It does not award a city-wide winner, compare unaudited success claims or guarantee that a particular surgical approach is right for you.

## Ten Ahmedabad surgical-care options

### Zydus Hospitals, Ahmedabad

**Location:** Zydus Hospitals Road, near Sola Bridge, SG Highway.

Zydus’s Ahmedabad page describes multiple surgical specialties and its GI department identifies laparoscopic and robotic gastrointestinal surgery. This supports a digestive-surgery consultation at the named institution, without establishing that every patient needs robotic assistance. Ask whether the proposed operation belongs with the GI team or another specialty. Confirm the exact campus because Zydus also operates a separate cancer hospital.

[Official information](https://zydushospitals.com/zydus-ahmedabad).

### Marengo CIMS Hospital

**Location:** Science City Road, Sola.

Marengo CIMS’s general-surgery page describes scheduled and emergency surgical services across several disciplines, including orthopaedic, oncological and gynaecological work. Its group contact information identifies the Sola location. Ask which department will take responsibility for your procedure and how preoperative assessment is coordinated. The number of specialties listed does not establish equal suitability for every complex operation.

[Official information](https://www.cims.org/general-surgery/).

### KD Hospital

**Location:** Vaishnodevi Circle, SG Highway.

KD’s general-surgery department lists breast-lump, thyroid, hernia and laparoscopic gallbladder procedures, among other services. Its official page distinguishes the main hospital at Vaishnodevi Circle from KD City Centre in Vastrapur. Ask where the actual operation will take place and which visits can be arranged locally. Make sure estimates refer to the same facility as the admission plan.

[Official information](https://kdhospital.co.in/specialities/general-surgery).

### Shalby Hospital, SG Highway

**Location:** Opposite Karnavati Club, SG Road.

The SG Shalby campus page lists bone-and-joint, cardiac, gastro, kidney, neuro and oncology services within its multi-specialty hospital. This is a specific location, distinct from Shalby’s Ghuma, Naroda and other Ahmedabad facilities. Identify the relevant surgical department before booking. Ask which specialist assesses the condition and whether any treatment or review would involve another campus.

[Official information](https://www.shalby.org/hospitals/sg-shalby-ahmedabad/).

### Sterling Hospitals, Gurukul

**Location:** Sterling Hospital Road, Memnagar.

Sterling’s Gurukul general-surgery page describes upper GI, hepatobiliary-pancreatic, colorectal and laparoscopic work, including input from other diagnostic specialists. The official contact page confirms Memnagar. Ask how a complex digestive diagnosis will be reviewed and which team explains the final plan. Distinguish Gurukul from Sterling Sindhu Bhavan when requesting an appointment or surgical estimate.

[Official information](https://www.sterlinghospitals.com/gurukul/specialities/gastrointestinal-general-surgery).

### HCG Hospitals, Ahmedabad

**Location:** Mithakhali, Ellisbridge.

HCG identifies this as its multi-specialty Ahmedabad hospital, with spine, orthopaedic, cardiac and critical-care services described. It is separate from HCG Aastha Cancer Centre in Sola and the Naroda day-care centre. Ask which facility treats the specific diagnosis and where surgery is planned. A network’s oncology reputation should not be used to assume all campuses provide identical cancer services.

[Official information](https://www.hcghospitals.com/our-centres/hcg-hospitals-ahmedabad).

### SAL Hospital and Medical Institute

**Location:** Drive In Road, Thaltej.

SAL’s surgical-services page describes general, laparoscopic, gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary, colorectal and emergency surgery, with surgical critical-care support. Its published address identifies the Thaltej campus. Ask whether your operation is a routine general-surgery procedure or requires a more focused team. Discuss the clinical reason for the proposed approach rather than selecting a technique from the procedure list.

[Official information](https://www.salhospital.com/medical-specialities/surgical-services/).

### U. N. Mehta Institute of Cardiology and Research Centre

**Location:** Civil Hospital campus, Asarwa.

This autonomous, government-supported cardiac institution describes adult and paediatric cardiac surgical programmes, including aortic and minimally invasive practice. It is a dedicated cardiac option rather than a general-surgery substitute. Ask for the relevant heart-surgery team and the investigations needed for your condition. Clarify the institution and building on the appointment because the larger Civil campus contains separate services.

[Official information](https://www.unmicrc.org/).

### Civil Hospital / BJ Medical College surgical services

**Location:** Asarwa, Ahmedabad.

BJ Medical College’s official general-surgery department describes basic and advanced surgical work, including laparoscopic, gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary and oncological procedures. This provides a public teaching-hospital route to assessment at the Civil Hospital setting. Ask about current registration, the appropriate unit and the exact clinic building. Confirm any scheme or financial eligibility through the authorised desk rather than assuming coverage from a general description.

[Official information](https://bjmcabd.edu.in/departments/general-surgery/).

### SVP Hospital, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Institute of Medical Sciences and Research

**Location:** Ellisbridge, Ahmedabad.

SVP’s official website and hospital-hosted general-surgery department document identify its surgical service and Ellisbridge location. The published roster is not treated here as a current appointment schedule. Ask the hospital which unit is appropriate, how to register and who will explain the proposed operation. This institution is distinct from the Asarwa Civil Hospital and U. N. Mehta entries.

[Official information](https://www.svphospital.com/assets/downloads/General_Surgery.pdf).

## Start with a specialist assessment

Bring the reports that led to the recommendation, original images where available, previous operation notes and a current medicine list. Explain the symptoms that matter most to you and what you hope treatment will change. The surgeon should be able to describe the proposed benefit, reasonable alternatives and the implications of waiting.

Ask whether the clinician’s current practice focuses on your condition. A general surgeon, cardiac surgeon, orthopaedic surgeon and surgical oncologist provide different expertise. For a complex procedure, ask how anaesthesia, critical care, radiology, pathology or another relevant specialty participates. A hospital listing all those departments does not mean each is needed or automatically involved.

If the plan mentions robotic or laparoscopic access, ask why it is appropriate and what other approaches are reasonable. The presence of equipment is useful information, but it does not establish that the technique offers a meaningful advantage in every case. Clarify what might require a change of approach during the operation and how that is discussed in consent.

## Make facilities relevant to the patient

Ask which services your operation is expected to require: a particular theatre setup, postoperative observation, rehabilitation, specialist nursing or intensive care. Confirm that the required support is available at the actual admission campus. A consultation at a convenient city centre can be useful, provided the operating and follow-up arrangements are explicit.

Discuss health conditions and medicines at the preoperative assessment. Cambridge University Hospitals’ patient information explains that medicine changes need individual clinical instructions and review. Give the team the full list, including supplements, and ask for a clear written plan. Do not stop a long-term medicine because another patient was told to stop theirs.

For outstation patients, ask how long local review may be needed, where follow-up occurs and what documents a clinician nearer home should receive. If language affects understanding, request an explanation or interpretation arrangement the patient can follow. The person undergoing surgery should be included in the decision, even when relatives handle transport and bills.

## Costs and coverage: compare a defined procedure

No fixed Ahmedabad surgery range is quoted because complete current, comparable estimates were not established. A heart operation and a minor lump removal cannot be compared using one city-wide number. Ask for an estimate naming the diagnosis, proposed operation, campus, room category and expected admission assumptions.

Clarify surgeon and anaesthesia fees, theatre costs, tests, medicines, consumables, implants where relevant, pathology, room or ICU allowance and initial reviews. Ask about exclusions and the basis for extra charges if the operation changes or the stay extends. If a second hospital proposes a different operation, understand that difference before treating the quotes as competing prices for the same treatment.

Check cashless network status, pre-authorisation, co-payment, room limits and non-payable items with the insurer or TPA. At public or scheme-linked services, ask the authorised desk about eligibility, documentation and any personal expense. Neither a hospital brand nor an old empanelment document establishes current coverage for every procedure.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Ahmedabad hospital is best for all surgery?

No single institution can be identified as best across unrelated procedures from this evidence. Compare the relevant specialist team, required support and a workable care pathway for the actual operation.

### Why is U. N. Mehta included separately from Civil Hospital?

It is a distinct cardiac institution on the Asarwa campus. Its role and appointment pathway differ from the general-surgery service associated with BJ Medical College and Civil Hospital.

### Is SVP Hospital the same as Asarwa Civil Hospital?

No. The SVP entry is in Ellisbridge. Confirm the institution, clinic and building before travelling or sending records.

### Are HCG Mithakhali and HCG Aastha one campus?

No. The listed Mithakhali entry is a multi-specialty hospital. HCG separately identifies its Aastha Cancer Centre at Sola. Book the facility appropriate to your diagnosis.

### Does KD City Centre necessarily provide the operation itself?

Do not assume that. KD’s official information distinguishes City Centre from its Vaishnodevi Circle hospital. Ask where assessment, surgery and reviews will take place.

### How should I assess a surgeon’s online profile?

Look for a current appointment and relevant practice, then ask the hospital to confirm who is expected to operate. Broad experience claims and promotional adjectives do not replace a personal consultation.

### Does minimally invasive surgery always mean a short stay?

No fixed stay can be promised for every operation and patient. Ask for expected admission and discharge criteria in your own circumstances, including what could require longer observation.

### Can a public hospital or government scheme be assumed free?

Confirm the specific service, eligibility and documentation with the authorised desk. Ask whether any tests, supplies or other expenses require personal payment.

### What should an outstation family arrange first?

Obtain the correct campus and appointment, share the requested records and discuss likely local review needs. Arrange accommodation and travel around the treating team’s advice rather than a generic recovery timetable.
