# Top 10 Hospitals in Delhi NCR for Major Surgeries: Infrastructure & Doctors

A patient comparing a gallbladder operation with a complex cancer resection is asking two different hospital questions. Both involve surgery, but the necessary team, postoperative support and follow-up may differ considerably. Delhi NCR adds another complication: a hospital advertised as being in Delhi may actually be in Gurugram or another neighbouring city. The address on the consultation slip matters as much as the brand name.

This guide to top hospitals in Delhi NCR for surgery brings together ten real campuses whose official sources describe relevant surgical services. It includes public teaching hospitals, a government general hospital and private multispecialty institutions. These are options to investigate, not positions in a league table. The order does not indicate clinical superiority, affordability or availability.

Use the profiles to identify the department that fits your diagnosis, then ask for an individual clinical assessment. The comparison also explains how to examine infrastructure claims, organise a second opinion and obtain estimates you can meaningfully compare. An appointment, operating slot or ICU bed must always be confirmed directly.

## Selection: what the evidence can and cannot establish

Official hospital and government pages were checked on 28 October 2026 for campus identity and published surgical departments. We prioritised identifiable units with a clear clinical service description over unsourced recommendation lists. Some profiles use a department page, which offers more useful detail than a chain-wide advertisement.

The research did not establish comparable, independently audited, risk-adjusted surgical outcomes across these hospitals. It also did not establish current waiting times, package prices or every accreditation certificate's validity. No star ratings or success percentages have been assigned. A department listing supports a service description; it cannot confirm that your exact operation is suitable or that a particular consultant is available today. Keep a dated note of the coordinator's answers so that changing arrangements can be rechecked before admission.

## Ten campuses with published surgical services

### 1. AIIMS, New Delhi

**Location:** New Delhi; the Surgical Disciplines department identifies its Surgery Block at the Masjid Moth campus. The [department's official introduction](https://www.aiims.edu/index.php/en/g_i_home?id=675) describes outpatient, inpatient and day-procedure services, with intensive-care and high-dependency support. Its training areas include minimal-access, bariatric and renal-transplant surgery.

For a referral, determine which department and clinic should receive your case rather than arriving with only a hospital name. The registration point and surgical assessment location may differ from another AIIMS centre. Ask how outside imaging will be reviewed, what records to bring and how follow-up is arranged if you travel from another state.

### 2. VMMC and Safdarjung Hospital

**Location:** New Delhi. Its [Surgery department](https://vmmc-sjh.mohfw.gov.in/surgery) publishes conventional and advanced laparoscopic services, surgical emergency care and focused clinics including thyroid, bariatric, breast and gastrointestinal/endoscopy services. The institution also lists separate surgical oncology and cardiothoracic departments.

This public teaching-hospital option should be approached through the specialty appropriate to the diagnosis. A referral for a suspected cancer may need a different pathway from a routine hernia consultation. Confirm the OPD building, registration process and documents required for your category. Ask who will explain the treatment decision and whom your family can contact during admission.

### 3. Lal Bahadur Shastri Hospital

**Location:** Khichripur, near Kalyanvas/Mayur Vihar, east Delhi. Its [government surgical-services page](https://lbsh.delhi.gov.in/lbsh/surgical-departments) lists gallbladder operations, laparoscopic hernia procedures, selected breast and bowel operations, anorectal surgery and surgical emergency facilities.

The published list makes this a specific east-Delhi general-surgery option, rather than a generic entry under a large corporate brand. It does not establish that every complex operation can be undertaken there for every patient. Discuss whether your illness falls within the current service and whether referral elsewhere is needed. Confirm elective registration separately from emergency attendance, and ask about investigation review before an admission date is fixed.

### 4. Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals

**Location:** Sarita Vihar, New Delhi. The [Delhi hospital page](https://www.apollohospitals.com/hospitals/apollo-hospitals-delhi) identifies the campus and its patient-support arrangements. Apollo's Delhi pages distinguish this unit from its other regional locations.

For a surgical consultation, specify Sarita Vihar and the required surgical specialty when requesting an appointment. A general chain enquiry is insufficient for a complicated referral. Ask the consultant to set out the intended operation, whether another specialty should contribute and where postoperative monitoring will occur. Patients comparing private estimates should request the room category, device or implant assumptions and expected follow-up charges in writing, rather than treating a preliminary coordinator quote as the final treatment plan.

### 5. Max Super Speciality Hospital, Saket

**Location:** Press Enclave Marg, Saket, New Delhi. Max's [campus page](https://www.maxhealthcare.in/hospital-network/max-super-speciality-hospital-saket) distinguishes East and West blocks and describes cardiac, cancer, transplant and general/minimal-access surgical services across the campus.

The block distinction is useful when coordinating several appointments or an admission. Confirm which unit houses the treating team and whether the estimate refers to the same unit. A liver-related referral, for example, should be routed to the relevant hepatobiliary/transplant service rather than an unspecified surgical appointment. Ask how the team shares investigation results, who provides the admission instructions and whether an outside pathology opinion needs formal review before surgery is offered.

### 6. Sir Ganga Ram Hospital

**Location:** New Delhi. Its [general and laparoscopic surgery institute](https://sgrh.com/departments/laparoscopic_surgery) lists gallbladder, hernia, breast, endocrine and gastrointestinal operations. The wider departmental directory identifies colorectal, minimal-access/bariatric, surgical gastroenterology and transplant services.

This range makes department selection particularly important. A pancreatic or rectal problem should not be assumed to follow the same route as a straightforward day procedure. Describe the diagnosis when seeking the appointment and check whether the specialist needs scans, endoscopy or pathology beforehand. If several departments are involved, ask who owns the combined plan and who will communicate changes to the family. Confirm the precise arrival point with the appointment office.

### 7. BLK-Max Super Speciality Hospital

**Location:** Pusa Road, Rajinder Nagar, New Delhi. The [official BLK-Max page](https://www.maxhealthcare.in/hospital-network/blk-max-super-speciality-hospital-delhi) describes a tertiary hospital with modular operating theatres and critical-care facilities, and lists robotic surgical technologies.

Equipment descriptions are a starting point for a clinical discussion, not proof that a robotic approach is preferable. Ask which platform is relevant to your diagnosis and who will perform the operation. Establish the conventional alternative, possible reasons to change approach and whether technology-related consumables alter the estimate. For a complex referral, also confirm that the intended postoperative unit and supporting specialty teams are part of the same written care plan.

### 8. Medanta – The Medicity

**Location:** Sector 38, Gurugram. The [Gurugram Gastrosciences page](https://www.medanta.org/hospitals-near-me/gurugram-hospital/speciality/gastroenterology) describes gastrointestinal, liver and pancreatic care, including minimally invasive and robotic procedures, with gastroenterology and surgical teams.

Patients considering an abdominal operation can ask how medical, endoscopic and surgical options will be reviewed together. The relevant issue is why an operation is recommended for your findings, not simply whether a robot is present. Because the campus is in Gurugram, plan travel and local support around actual appointments. Ask whether follow-up can be coordinated with your home-city clinician and which symptoms would require assessment at the operating centre.

### 9. Artemis Hospital

**Location:** Gurugram. Artemis publishes [robotic surgery](https://www.artemishospitals.com/procedures/robotic-surgery) and [laparoscopic surgery](https://www.artemishospitals.com/procedures/laparoscopic-surgery) information, describing applications across urology, gynaecology, oncology and gastrointestinal care.

The distinction between a surgical approach and a diagnosis remains essential. A procedure-specific consultation should establish whether minimally invasive access is appropriate and what alternatives exist. Request an explanation of surgeon experience with the proposed operation, rather than relying on the hospital's general technology description. Families travelling across NCR should also discuss how many reviews are likely, who handles an unscheduled concern and whether a local review can be arranged if returning to Gurugram is difficult.

### 10. Fortis Memorial Research Institute

**Location:** Gurugram. FMRI's [robotic-assisted surgery page](https://www.fortishealthcare.com/centres-of-excellence/hospitals/robotic-assisted-surgery-fortis-memorial-research-institute-gurgaon) identifies services involving general surgery, urology, gynaecological oncology, surgical oncology and kidney transplantation. Its hospital page separately describes critical-care infrastructure.

For an oncology or transplant referral, confirm the exact specialty pathway and whether assessment requires a multidisciplinary meeting or additional authorisation. The presence of several services does not establish eligibility for a specific treatment. Ask how the proposed approach affects recovery, what circumstances could change it and how the hospital handles escalation after discharge. Verify that the appointment, admission location and financial paperwork all name the same campus.

## Build a shortlist around the care pathway

Begin with the written diagnosis and proposed operation. Ask your clinician what supporting services might be needed, such as pathology review, rehabilitation, intensive care or another surgical specialty. Then select two or three campuses capable of assessing that particular problem. Comparing ten appointments rarely helps as much as comparing two complete clinical plans.

For each opinion, carry the same imaging, reports, prescription list and previous operation details. Ask the doctor to distinguish what is known from what further testing must establish. If opinions differ, identify the reason: diagnosis, stage of illness, operative risk or patient preference. A different recommendation is a reason for clarification, not automatic evidence that either team is wrong.

Discuss practical access as well. A convenient initial consultation can lead to repeated cross-city travel for testing, dressings or rehabilitation. Consider a caregiver's availability and the plan for urgent review after returning home. Do not delay emergency assessment while comparing elective hospital options.

## Obtain an estimate that matches the operation

Request the clinical plan before requesting a price comparison. A useful estimate identifies the planned procedure, surgeon and anaesthesia fees, room category, devices, consumables, investigations and assumptions about length of stay. Ask what happens financially if the operation changes or additional monitoring is needed.

Keep estimates from different hospitals in the same format. A deposit is not a total cost, and a headline package may omit pathology, rehabilitation or later consultations. No current comparable prices were verified for this guide, so a numerical price range would give a false impression of precision.

Check cashless approval with both the hospital and insurer. Record the proposed admission date, approved procedure and any exclusions or patient contribution. Public-sector access, scheme eligibility and appointment routes should be confirmed through the institution; do not assume that being listed in a directory establishes entitlement or an immediate operating slot.

## Questions for the appointment or admission desk

1. Which campus, department and consultant will review this diagnosis?
2. Can existing scans and pathology be assessed before repeat testing?
3. Who explains alternatives and obtains informed consent?
4. What postoperative monitoring might my condition require?
5. Is rehabilitation provided locally or through a referral?
6. Which charges are outside the written estimate?
7. What insurer approval is still outstanding?
8. Who handles a problem after discharge or after I return home?

## Frequently asked questions

### Does “top ten” mean these hospitals have the best results?

No. The official SEO title is retained, but the article presents a non-ranked shortlist. The research did not establish a common audited outcomes dataset from which a defensible performance ranking could be calculated.

### Is a Gurugram hospital considered part of Delhi NCR?

Yes, Gurugram is included in this regional guide. It is a separate city, so check the complete address rather than assuming your appointment is in New Delhi. Travel planning should follow the actual campus.

### Should every surgery be performed at a large tertiary hospital?

The appropriate setting depends on the procedure and individual risk. Ask your treating clinician what resources are needed. A long equipment list alone does not establish that a setting is either necessary or suitable.

### Can I choose by the lowest estimate?

Only after comparing the clinical plan and inclusions. Different procedures, devices or room categories can produce very different quotes. Ask for written clarification when one estimate appears substantially cheaper.

### Is robotic availability a reason to reject another hospital?

Not by itself. Ask whether a robotic approach offers a meaningful advantage for the proposed operation and whether an appropriate alternative is available. The surgeon should explain the recommendation in your circumstances.

### Are doctor names deliberately omitted?

Yes. The guide identifies services rather than promising an individual clinician's current availability. Verify the treating consultant's qualifications, registration, role and operating location directly before making a decision.

### What does a hospital accreditation logo tell me?

It is a prompt to check the current certificate, unit and scope. A logo does not establish a particular surgeon's results. This article has not independently validated every hospital's accreditation status.

### What should an outstation patient organise first?

Obtain a confirmed appointment and a list of records to bring before booking an extended stay. Ask whether reports can be reviewed remotely, how long evaluation may take and which follow-up visits require attendance.
