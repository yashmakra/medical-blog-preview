# Top 10 Hospitals in Bengaluru for Surgery: Patient Evaluation Guide

Before comparing hospitals in Bengaluru, put the proposed operation into words you understand. Is the recommendation for a joint replacement, a bowel resection, an eye procedure or a diagnostic operation? Have the findings established the diagnosis, or is further assessment needed? The answers determine which specialist and supporting services should be on your shortlist.

This guide to top hospitals in Bengaluru for surgery uses official sources to describe ten identifiable campuses. It includes established multispecialty institutions, a teaching hospital and services in different parts of the city. Bengaluru's hospital networks contain many branches; a department advertised by a group should not automatically be assumed to operate at every address.

The aim is a better patient evaluation, not a promotional ranking. The profiles identify useful starting points and questions to take to a consultation. Later sections explain how to compare recommendations, decide what further information is needed and plan follow-up around travel and family support. The appropriate choice depends on your condition and care requirements, rather than a familiar name, a robot or a convenient appointment alone.

## How the ten options were selected

On 28 October 2026, official campus, department and institutional pages were reviewed for location and surgical-service information. Preference was given to sources linking a specialty to an identifiable hospital. Where a broader directory is used, the limitation is made clear and the exact treatment must be confirmed with the campus.

This is not an exhaustive survey of Bengaluru hospitals. Inclusion does not establish comparative clinical performance, and absence does not imply poor care. No comparable risk-adjusted outcomes, live admission capacity or complete current tariff dataset was established. Accreditation and individual surgeon availability have not been independently certified for this guide. Patients should reconfirm those details rather than treating an online logo or roster as a permanent assurance.

## Hospitals and the evaluation routes they describe

### 1. Manipal Hospital, Old Airport Road

**Location:** HAL Old Airport Road, Bengaluru. Its [branch-specific general-surgery page](https://www.manipalhospitals.com/oldairportroad/specialities/general-surgery/) describes a multidisciplinary surgical service working with anaesthesiology, imaging and other specialists. The campus is distinct from Manipal's Whitefield, Hebbal and other city units.

Ask which service should assess the actual diagnosis and whether existing reports can be reviewed before new investigations are requested. If a specialist consults at more than one Manipal site, confirm where the operation and subsequent reviews would occur. An estimate should identify the same campus and approach as the clinical recommendation. Do not transfer assumptions about equipment, dates or insurer access from another branch.

### 2. Apollo Hospitals, Bannerghatta Road

**Location:** Bannerghatta Road, opposite the IIM area. The [official campus page](https://www.apollohospitals.com/hospitals/apollo-hospitals-bannerghatta-road) identifies the Bengaluru unit and its multispecialty services. Apollo's regional information also distinguishes Bannerghatta from Jayanagar and Sheshadripuram.

Use the branch name when requesting a surgical assessment. Ask whether your diagnosis belongs with general surgery or a subspecialty, and which records are needed for that appointment. Where a particular technology is proposed, request a clinical explanation of its role and an estimate covering its consumables. The hospital's overall service breadth should not be taken as confirmation that a particular operation is appropriate for you or available on your preferred date.

### 3. Fortis Hospital, Bannerghatta Road

**Location:** Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru. Fortis publishes both a [campus description](https://www.fortishealthcare.com/location/fortis-hospital-bg-road-bangalore) and a general-surgery listing tied to this hospital. These are more specific than a network-wide page advertising an operation.

A patient comparing the two Bannerghatta Road institutions should keep their records and quotes clearly labelled. They are separate hospitals despite the similar locality description. Ask who will lead your assessment, which supporting specialties are relevant and how unexpected postoperative needs would be managed. If several opinions are being sought, compare the reasoning for the treatment and the follow-up plan before comparing the headline price.

### 4. Aster CMI Hospital

**Location:** New Airport Road, Sahakara Nagar/Hebbal area. [Aster CMI's campus page](https://www.asterhospitals.in/hospitals/aster-cmi-bangalore) describes a multispecialty institution providing care through to complex specialties, with surgical gastroenterology, neurosciences, orthopaedics and transplant information in its service directory.

Confirm that the specialist appointment and proposed procedure relate to CMI rather than a different Aster hospital. For a complex abdominal or neurological referral, ask how the relevant teams review the diagnosis together and what information they need in advance. Clarify whether rehabilitation, dietary review or repeated appointments form part of the likely pathway so that family support can be organised realistically.

### 5. Mazumdar Shaw Medical Centre

**Location:** Narayana Health City, Bommasandra, Hosur Road. The [official hospital profile](https://www.narayanahealth.org/hospitals-clinics/bangalore/mazumdar-shaw-medical-centre-bommasandra) identifies the centre and lists surgical-related specialties including neuroscience, orthopaedics and robotic services.

Narayana Health City contains distinct institutions and service areas. Ask for the full name of the centre and department receiving your referral, rather than relying on “Narayana” alone. For an outstation patient, confirm where investigations, admission and follow-up take place. If more than one team will contribute to the plan, ask who coordinates the assessments and how the final recommendation will be documented for you and your local clinician.

### 6. Sakra World Hospital

**Location:** Devarabeesanahalli, Outer Ring Road, in the Marathahalli area. Sakra's [official site](https://www.sakraworldhospital.com/) lists robotic-assisted orthopaedic, urological, gastrointestinal, cardiac and gynaecological services. Its contact page provides the specific address.

The different programmes should not be treated as a single interchangeable technique. Ask which service is relevant to the proposed operation, what alternative access is available and how the choice affects the care pathway. Patients using Outer Ring Road for appointments should leave room for repeat visits and discharge transport. The clinical team should decide any travel restrictions; a published map cannot establish when you will be ready to return home.

### 7. St. John's Medical College Hospital

**Location:** Sarjapur Road near the Hosur Road junction, Koramangala area. The [institution's hospital portal](https://www.stjohns.in/hospital/) and institutional location information distinguish the main hospital from its Brigade Meadows facility. It is part of St. John's National Academy of Health Sciences.

For a surgical referral, establish the correct specialty registration route and whether a previous referral or report is needed. A teaching-hospital pathway may involve several members of a clinical team; ask who supervises the decision and how the planned treatment will be explained. Verify which building and clinic your appointment refers to. Main-campus information should not be assumed to apply to a separate outreach or satellite facility.

### 8. Ramaiah Memorial Hospital

**Location:** Bengaluru. Ramaiah's [general-surgery department page](https://msrmh.com/general-surgery/) describes a tertiary referral service, major and minor surgery, trauma support and advanced minimal-access procedures. Listed conditions include hernias, gallbladder disease and abdominal trauma.

The published service scope gives a useful starting point for a general surgical opinion. Ask whether your referral needs a particular subspecialist and how appointments relate to investigation review. Distinguish the hospital named on your appointment from other institutions carrying the Ramaiah name. Obtain its full address and registration instructions directly, particularly if a referral letter uses an older institutional description or an abbreviated campus name.

### 9. SPARSH Hospital, Infantry Road

**Location:** Infantry Road, central Bengaluru. The [campus profile](https://www.sparshhospital.com/hospitals/sparsh-hospital-infantry-road/) identifies the hospital, while SPARSH's general-surgery directory specifically lists Infantry Road among its locations for general and laparoscopic care.

This branch detail matters because SPARSH operates several hospitals across the city. Ask which consultant and operating unit will manage your case and whether the required supporting services are at that site. A central consultation location may be convenient, but confirm the actual admission address before finalising arrangements. For a planned operation, request a written pathway covering preoperative review, admission and rehabilitation rather than treating the appointment as the complete treatment plan.

### 10. Gleneagles BGS Hospital, Kengeri

**Location:** Kengeri, Bengaluru. The [branch-specific general/minimal-access surgery page](https://www.gleneagleshospitals.co.in/bengaluru/kengeri/speciality/general-surgery-minimal-access-surgery) describes laparoscopic procedures, including gallbladder, hernia, appendix and colorectal services. Its campus pages also distinguish Kengeri from other Gleneagles locations.

For a western-Bengaluru referral, check whether general surgery or a more specialised department should review the records. The hospital's broader descriptions of transplant, spine or other services are not an individual recommendation. Ask how the proposed operation would be planned, where postoperative review occurs and what contact route is available if you develop a concern after leaving the campus.

## What a useful patient evaluation should resolve

### Is the diagnosis sufficiently clear?

Ask the doctor which findings explain the symptoms and which uncertainties remain. An imaging report, a suspected diagnosis and a confirmed tissue diagnosis are different kinds of information. If more tests are recommended, ask what decision each test could change. Do not buy a fixed test package merely because another patient had one.

### What are the reasonable treatment choices?

Request the reason for recommending an operation, the alternatives and the consequences of waiting. The appropriate discussion varies between a routine elective condition and a time-sensitive diagnosis. If the doctor considers treatment urgent, ask them to explain the clinical reason and how the next steps will be coordinated.

### Which risks and support needs are individual to you?

Give the team an accurate medicine list and disclose previous operations, anaesthetic problems and existing illnesses. Ask whether another specialist should contribute before a decision. Do not independently change medicines or begin a fasting regimen because a date is being considered. Obtain patient-specific written instructions from the responsible team.

### How will you judge the proposed result?

Define the outcome you hope treatment will improve: symptoms, mobility, disease control or another goal. Ask what limitations may remain and how progress is assessed. A technology name or a general “success” percentage cannot replace a discussion of realistic goals, complications and the follow-up needed to understand the result.

## Compare costs and follow-up together

A useful quote follows a clinical recommendation. It identifies the operation, access approach, professional and anaesthetic fees, room category, implant/device assumptions and expected monitoring. Ask which investigations, medicines, consumables and later reviews fall outside the estimate. If two hospitals propose different operations, seek clarification before treating the prices as comparable.

For insured treatment, verify the named campus and procedure with the insurer and hospital desk. Record authorisation conditions and patient contributions. A hospital appearing on a network list does not mean that every service is automatically approved under your policy.

Consider the practical cost of repeated journeys as well. Bengaluru patients may need imaging, pre-anaesthetic review and postoperative visits on different days. Discuss appointment coordination, caregiver availability and whether some reviews can happen closer to home. No current comparable price dataset was verified for this guide; a fabricated rupee range would obscure these individual differences.

## Questions to take to the specialist

1. What evidence establishes the diagnosis and need for surgery?
2. Which alternatives are reasonable in my circumstances?
3. What additional test would change the decision?
4. Who will perform and supervise the operation?
5. What patient-specific risks require further assessment?
6. Is the proposed technology necessary or optional?
7. What does the estimate exclude?
8. Which follow-up visits must be at the operating hospital?
9. What symptoms require urgent reassessment?

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the hospitals listed in order of quality?

No. They form a sourced, non-ranked shortlist. Different patients require different specialties, and the research did not establish comparable audited results from which to calculate an overall surgical quality ranking.

### Why is the branch name repeated so often?

Several hospital groups have multiple Bengaluru locations. The treating team, equipment, admission process and insurer arrangements may differ. The exact campus should match across your appointment, care plan, estimate and authorisation.

### Can I bring imaging from another hospital?

Ask the specialist whether the images, report and date are suitable for the decision being considered. Carry the actual image files where available. A repeat investigation should have a clinical reason rather than being assumed automatically unnecessary or mandatory.

### Is an assessment at a teaching hospital different?

The consultation may involve a team and a registration pathway. Ask who supervises the treatment recommendation and operation, and how consent, communication and later reviews are organised. Confirm the specific service directly with the institution.

### Should convenience determine my final choice?

Convenience matters for appointments and recovery, but first establish whether the hospital can assess and support the proposed treatment. An easier journey is valuable when the required clinical services and a suitable specialist are available.

### Does a robot make surgery the right choice?

No technology establishes the indication for an operation. Ask the specialist to explain the diagnosis, alternatives and reason for the proposed approach. The decision should remain specific to your case and goals.

### How do I compare two different recommendations?

Ask whether they differ because of diagnosis, urgency, anticipated benefit or patient risk. Share the relevant records with both clinicians. Request an explanation of the uncertainty rather than assuming that the more extensive treatment is automatically better.

### Are accreditation certificates checked for every hospital?

No. This article verifies published service information but does not certify current accreditation status. If it influences your choice, ask for the exact unit, scope, issue/expiry dates and a current independent listing.

### When should I arrange discharge transport?

Discuss likely requirements during planning, then follow the treating team's actual discharge advice. Do not assume you can drive yourself or travel long distances immediately after a procedure. The operation and recovery determine the plan.
