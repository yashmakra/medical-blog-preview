# Top 10 Hospitals in Gurugram for Advanced Robotic & Laparoscopic Surgeries

“Robotic” and “laparoscopic” appear frequently in hospital advertisements, but neither term tells you whether you need surgery or which operation is appropriate. When comparing the top hospitals in Gurugram for surgery, begin with the diagnosis and the treatment goal. The access method should follow that decision and a discussion of reasonable alternatives.

Gurugram offers several hospitals advertising robotic programmes and others describing conventional laparoscopic practice. Those are useful options to compare, provided the operation, surgeon and admission campus are clearly identified. A machine listed on a website is not evidence that the newest technique is necessary, available on your date or better for every patient.

This guide presents ten sourced hospitals or campuses in no performance order. It includes Manesar as a clearly labelled Gurugram-district option and Sethi as a laparoscopic comparator without a verified robotic claim. The purpose is to help patients ask focused questions about technique, team experience, supporting care, costs and recovery. It is not a ranking of technology or a promise of faster discharge, less pain or a particular clinical result.

## Understand what the surgeon controls

The US FDA explains that robotically assisted surgical systems allow a surgeon to control instruments using computer-assisted technology. The device does not independently perform the operation. It can assist minimally invasive work and complex tasks, but suitability, training and the risks and benefits of alternatives should be discussed with the treating clinician.

Laparoscopy generally uses a camera and instruments through small incisions. Robotic assistance is one way to carry out selected minimally invasive procedures. Open access may also be appropriate. Ask the surgeon what is being done inside the body, not only how large the incisions may be, and whether the recommended approach changes the expected clinical benefit.

If cancer is involved, ask how the proposed operation fits the disease-specific treatment plan. Technology alone cannot establish complete removal or better long-term outcomes. Clarify whether pathology review or multidisciplinary discussion should occur before fixing a date. Avoid allowing a package label to replace a diagnosis-led decision.

## Questions about experience and contingency plans

Ask who is expected to operate, their current practice with the proposed procedure and who assists at the bedside. A statement about total career experience does not necessarily describe recent experience with your operation or the specific system. The hospital should be able to explain the relevant team without pressuring you to choose from marketing material.

Discuss what could require a switch to another technique and how that possibility is covered in consent. Ask what happens if the equipment is unavailable on the planned day. A contingency plan should preserve the clinical goal and patient safety, with the alternatives explained before admission wherever possible.

Check anaesthesia assessment, expected postoperative observation and the clinical contact route after discharge. For Manesar or a campus across the city from home, consider how reviews and unexpected concerns would be handled. A short advertised stay is useful only if discharge criteria and support at home are appropriate for the patient.

## Compare the full cost of the same operation

No Gurugram robotic-surgery price range is quoted because current complete, comparable tariffs were not established. A procedure-specific estimate is more useful than a headline technology surcharge. Request the same proposed operation, room category and admission assumptions from each hospital you are seriously considering.

Ask whether robotic instruments, platform charges, imaging, consumables and any implant are separately billed. Clarify surgeon and anaesthesia fees, theatre costs, medicines, pathology, room or ICU allowance and initial follow-up. Request the likely charging basis if the operation changes or admission lasts longer. Compare a conventional laparoscopic option when the clinician considers it reasonable.

Ask the insurer or TPA whether your proposed approach and procedure require specific authorisation, and check room limits, co-payment and non-payable items. A general assurance that the hospital offers cashless care does not settle your policy’s coverage. Obtain a dated estimate and keep a written record of material exclusions and personal payment obligations.

## Questions to take to the hospital

- What clinical advantage is expected from robotic assistance?
- What reasonable alternative approaches exist?
- Who controls and assists with the system?
- What if the platform is unavailable?
- Which technology charges are excluded?

## Frequently asked questions

### Does every hospital in this list offer robotic surgery?

No. Sethi is included on verified conventional and laparoscopic evidence. Other entries have robotic service evidence, but the exact procedure and platform need direct confirmation.

### Does the robot make the surgical decisions?

The systems discussed here are surgeon-controlled assistance. Ask the surgeon to explain the system’s role and the team’s responsibilities for the proposed operation.

### Is a robotic operation always better than laparoscopy?

That cannot be assumed. Ask about the expected benefit, limitations and costs for the particular procedure and your circumstances, including reasonable alternatives.

### Are joint-replacement and abdominal surgical robots the same?

They serve different functions. Confirm that the hospital’s claimed technology is relevant to your operation rather than relying on a generic robotic-surgery label.

### Are Fortis FMRI and Fortis Manesar the same hospital?

No. They are separate campuses, and Manesar is explicitly identified here as a Gurugram-district option. Confirm where the surgeon consults and where admission is planned.

### Where are Manipal and Marengo’s listed campuses?

Manipal is at Palam Vihar; Marengo is at Sector 56 on Golf Course Extension Road. Their official location information should be used when planning appointments and reviews.

### What if an operation needs to be converted to open surgery?

Ask the surgeon to explain the possible reasons, consent process and implications for recovery and cost before admission. A change in approach may be necessary to complete the intended treatment safely.

### Can insurance approval be assumed for a robot-assisted procedure?

No. Confirm your policy, procedure and approach with the insurer or TPA. Ask whether any additional authorisation, sublimit or excluded charge applies.

### How soon will I recover after keyhole surgery?

Recovery depends on the operation, your health and the course after surgery. Request individual advice about activity, work, travel and assistance at home; no universal timetable applies to every minimally invasive procedure.

## Prepare two comparable consultation summaries

If you seek opinions at two hospitals, note the diagnosis each team is treating, the operation proposed and the reasons for its approach. Record whether the difference concerns the treatment itself, the method of access or additional support. This makes a disagreement easier to discuss than a comparison of package names alone.

Ask both teams what information is still missing. One may need original imaging or pathology review before giving a definitive recommendation. Clarify whether that review could change the proposed operation and what delay is clinically acceptable. Avoid interpreting a faster quotation as proof that the assessment is more complete.

Finally, take the same financial assumptions to each billing desk. Ask for a proposal that specifies the actual campus, operation, room category, anticipated stay and technology charges. Keep the written estimate with the clinical recommendation so a relative can understand what has and has not been agreed. These records also help clarify changes before a deposit is paid.
